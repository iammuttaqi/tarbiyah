About The Platform

Your structured path to Islamic Knowledge.

Tarbiya is a comprehensive online learning app and website designed to impart authentic Islamic knowledge in a structured, easy-to-follow format. Built for both children and adults, it serves as a digital gateway to learning the Deen based strictly on the Qur’an, the authentic Sunnah, and the methodology of the Salaf.

We recognize that every student begins their journey at a different point. Therefore, Tarbiya is organized into levels of understanding, allowing users to progress from foundational principles to more detailed studies at a pace that suits them.

Why Tarbiya Exists

"What I love for my children, I want for every Muslim child in the world too."

When it was time for my children to start learning Islam, I was lost. I did not know where to start, and how to approach educating my children on Islam. My search for a basic, structured course on authentic Quran and Sunnah that my children could do for even 15 minutes a day yielded little results.

And, like most parents, I did not want my children being raised on YouTube and social media algorithms. So, I built the Tarbiya platform. It aims to educate Muslims (not just children) on everything about Islam based on your level no matter your age or background.

Our Learning Methodology

A deliberate, thoughtful approach to seeking knowledge that protects the learner from confusion and overwhelm.

Rooted in Revelation

Every lesson is drawn from authentic Islamic sources—the Qur'an and Sunnah—and classical scholarship upon the understanding of the Salaf.

Structured, Not Scattered

Topics are taught in a deliberate sequence, building foundations first and guiding learners step by step without overwhelming them.

Knowledge with Practice

True learning is tied to reflection and action. We focus not just on memorization, but on applying the Deen to everyday life.

Comprehensive Curriculum

Explore the vast sciences of the Deen broken down into digestible, structured modules for every level.

Simple, Engaging Learning

Tarbiya uses simple, interactive activities to help learners understand and remember what they learn.

Through crosswords, puzzles, quizzes, and word-matching exercises, learning stays clear, focused, and meaningful without feeling overwhelming.
