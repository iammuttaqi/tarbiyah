About Tarbiya

Building a structured path to authentic Islamic knowledge for every Muslim

Our Mission

To provide every Muslim—regardless of age, background, or level—with clear, structured, and authentic Islamic education rooted in the Quran, Sunnah, and the understanding of the Salaf.

Why Tarbiya?

When it was time for my children to start learning Islam, I was lost. I did not know where to start, and how to approach educating my children on Islam. My search for a basic, structured course on authentic Quran and Sunnah that my children could do for even 15 minutes a day came up empty.

And, like most parents, I did not want my children being raised on Youtube and social media. So, I built the Tarbiya platform. It aims to educate Muslims (not just children) on everything about Islam based on your level no matter your age or background.

What I love for my children, I want for every Muslim child in the world too.

Our Core Values

Authentic Knowledge

Every lesson is rooted in the Quran, authentic Sunnah, and the understanding of the righteous predecessors (Salaf).

Structured Learning

A clear, step-by-step curriculum that builds knowledge gradually, making Islam accessible to learners at every level.

Application Focused

Learning is not just about memorization—it's about understanding, reflection, and applying Islamic teachings in daily life.

Who We Serve

Parents

New Muslims

Youth

Every Muslim

Whether you're seeking to deepen your knowledge or refresh the basics, Tarbiya meets you where you are.
